DATA & METHODOLOGY

Evidence before placement.

Nodes: MeshCore public map. Only type-2 repeaters with valid advertised coordinates are included. Last-advert dates describe reported advertisements, not online status. The regional feed is cached for up to 15 minutes and never silently replaced with sample data.

Topology: eligible nodes share the selected advertised frequency, bandwidth, spreading factor and coding rate and fall inside the recency window. Dotted links use a user-set distance limit, not a propagation model. Imported directed observations are drawn separately. Proximity groups and their minimum-distance bridges are hypotheses; other radio profiles and stale nodes are excluded.

Mapped infrastructure: OpenStreetMap contributors through a bounded Overpass query. Coverage and tagging vary. Amateur-radio markers appear only when a public map feature is explicitly tagged; this is not a complete repeater or operator directory. A mapped tower, rooftop or host structure is not an offer of access or permission.

Basemaps: Street uses OpenStreetMap tile servers; Topographic and Aerial use Esri ArcGIS Online services with attribution shown on the map. External basemaps are online-only; Field Grid is the offline basemap.

Terrain: Open-Elevation ground data is sampled at 15 points on each of two paths per candidate and cached for up to 24 hours. Profiles show straight-line clearance with Earth curvature (4/3 effective radius) and 60% first Fresnel zone. Sparse samples, dataset resolution and obfuscated repeater locations can miss obstacles.

Candidate points do not establish physical site access, land cover, structural suitability or successful bidirectional links. Trees, buildings, RF noise, equipment losses and land access remain unresolved. Ground coordinates are sent to the elevation provider only when you run a terrain screen.
